# Reference: JA Africa (ja-africa.org)

Used as the structural and tonal reference for the Afrimmak Design System v1
at the user's request: *"I like that website and would like to emulate it for mine."*

## Page structure (in order)

1. **Top nav** — logo + 5 links (About Us, Our Work, Programs, Impact, Get Involved) + Donate CTA.
2. **Hero** — Big headline ("Empowering Africa's Next Generation"), sub, Learn More CTA, embedded YouTube on the right.
3. **Progress in Numbers** — 4-up stats grid with custom outline SVG icons, big figures (1,296,854 Learning Experiences / 2,204 Institutions / 2,684 Teachers / 3,547 Volunteers) and short captions.
4. **What We Do** — 3 program pillars (Work Readiness / Entrepreneurship Education / Financial Health), each a circular illustrated SVG tile with "Learn More" link.
5. **Global Recognition** — awards / honours on dark background.
6. **Partners** — supporter logos grid.
7. **Back a Budding Entrepreneur** — donate CTA with photo collage of young entrepreneurs.
8. **Newsletter** — subscribe.
9. **Footer** — Quick Links, Connect With Us, Legal, language switcher.

## Voice & tone

- **Mission-driven, formal-ish, partnership-focused.** Not startup-bro.
- Sample copy: *"We empower Africa's youth with the skillset and mindset to shape their futures and transform their communities."*
- Verbs: **empower, equip, prepare, inspire, deliver, train, partner**. (Not: ship, build, hustle.)
- Phrase patterns: "next generation of leaders, entrepreneurs, and change-makers"; "skillset and mindset"; "bridge the gap between school learning and real-world workplace skills".
- Numbers are exact and large ("1,296,854" not "over 1 million"). Trust through specificity.
- POV: "We" (the org) and "young people" / "Africa's youth" — rarely "you".
- Headings: **Title Case** for section labels, **Sentence case** is fine for body.
- Honours: "**honours**" / "**organisations**" — British/Commonwealth spelling.

## Visual elements to lift

- Stat tiles with illustrated outline icons (not Lucide stroke icons — these are
  custom domain-specific illustrations: a book, a building, people, a volunteer).
- Three-pillar program cards as **circular illustrations** rather than card+icon.
- Photo collage layout for the donate section (5 staggered photos).
- Sections separated by generous whitespace, not heavy dividers.

## What we KEEP from Afrimmak v0

- Blue/green/red palette (don't switch to JA's red/yellow).
- Ethnocentric brand display font.
- The Afrimmak logo as the locked mark.
- 8pt spacing grid, pill buttons, soft navy-tinted shadows.

## What we CHANGE in v1

- Replace "Spark Fellowship / Builder Grant / Alumni Network" with
  **Work Readiness / Entrepreneurship Education / Financial Health**
  (mapping to JA's three pillars).
- Replace "Apply for Cohort '25" hero CTA with a softer **"Learn More"** plus
  an embedded **video** on the right.
- Replace "Apply now" overlay with a simpler **Donate** flow and **Newsletter**
  signup (matches JA Africa's primary conversion goals).
- Soften voice toward JA Africa's tone (still optimistic, less "ship").
